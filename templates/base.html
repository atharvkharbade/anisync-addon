What's New in AniSync
BETA v1.4.0 (Latest Beta)
  • Multi-Tracker Recommendations: AI recommendations now analyze watch history across all your connected accounts (MyAnimeList, AniList, and Simkl).

v1.3.0 — Simkl, Sorting & Watched Tags
  • Simkl Tracker Support: Full Simkl integration for real-time progress sync, combined watchlists, and logo badges alongside MAL and AniList.
